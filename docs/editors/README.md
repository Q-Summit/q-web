# Editor handbook

How divisions edit [q-summit.com](https://q-summit.com), written for non-developers. Everything here happens in the Payload admin interface in your browser. You never need GitHub, this repository, or any developer tools.

## Your account

- Your login is personal: one account per person, never shared.
- It is scoped to your division: you see and edit the content your division owns (for example, the partner division edits partners and jobs, PR edits speakers and the FAQ).
- Account questions (lost password, missing permissions, new members) go to your division's approver, who routes them to the maintainers.

## From draft to live

1. **Edit and save.** Your change is saved as a draft. The live website does not change.
2. **Preview.** Check how the draft will look before anyone else sees it.
3. **Submit for approval.** An approver in your division reviews the draft.
4. **Publish.** Only approvers publish. The site then rebuilds automatically, and the change is live within about 5 minutes.

Nothing goes live without the approval step, and a failed rebuild never breaks the live site: the old version simply stays up.

## When something goes wrong

- You cannot find or edit something you think you should: ask your division's approver first.
- The live site shows something broken (layout, dead link, outdated page that will not update): tell your division's approver, who passes it to the maintainers, or file a bug on GitHub if you have an account there.
